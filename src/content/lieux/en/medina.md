## A city within the city

Listed as a UNESCO World Heritage site in 1988, the medina of Sousse is one of the most complete examples of a coastal town from the first centuries of Islam. Its ramparts, raised in 859 and more than two kilometres long, enclose a maze of white-and-blue lanes, covered souks, mosques and caravanserais.

## A simple route

1. Enter through **Bab el Bhar**, the sea gate, and climb towards the Great Mosque square and the ribat.
2. Follow **Rue d’Angleterre** and the souks: El Reba (fabrics), El Caïd, the jewellers.
3. Stop at the **Sidi Bouraoui café**, under its blue chairs, in front of the zawiya.
4. Climb to the **kasbah** for the archaeological museum and the view.
5. Come back down through **Bab el Finga** and the western ramparts.

## Tips

The souks close on Sunday afternoons. Before 10am, the medina still belongs to its residents.
