## The story of the building

In the heart of the medina, El Kobba is a gem of Arab-Muslim architecture. Its zigzag dome, dated to the eleventh century, is unique in Tunisia. The building served as an inn (fondouk) for passing merchants and travellers, then as a café, before becoming a museum of Sousse’s trades and crafts.

## The collections

Pottery, traditional jewellery, costumes and old tools of the region’s artisans. Reconstructed scenes — a wedding, a hammam, a workshop — bring daily life of the past back to the surface.

## The view

From the museum’s terrace, the whole medina: narrow lanes, white domes and minarets.
