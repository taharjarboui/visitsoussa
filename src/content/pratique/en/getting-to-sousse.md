## By air

- **Enfidha-Hammamet airport** (NBE), 45 km north: charter and low-cost flights from Europe. Taxi or shuttle, 40 min.
- **Monastir airport** (MIR), 20 km south: charter flights. Taxi, 25 min; the Sahel metro also links the airport to Sousse.
- **Tunis-Carthage airport** (TUN), 140 km: scheduled flights. Then train, shared taxi or car, about 2 hours.

## By train

The Tunis–Sousse line (SNCFT) has several trains a day, 2 to 2.5 hours. The station is in the city centre, five minutes from the medina.

## By shared taxi (louage)

Louages leave Moncef Bey station in Tunis as soon as they are full: 1h45, for a few dinars.

## By car

The A1 motorway links Tunis to Sousse in 1.5 hours (toll).
