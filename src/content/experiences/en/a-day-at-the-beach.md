From the first rays of sun, the beach comes alive: children’s laughter, coloured parasols opening, the smell of the sea. Swim in calm, clear water, try a jet-ski or parasailing, then eat grilled fish and salade méchouia at a seafront restaurant. In the afternoon, a nap under a palm tree or a game of beach volleyball. And in the evening, the sky turns pink and orange over the Mediterranean.

Whether you come with family, friends or as a couple, a day on Sousse’s beach is a day that recharges body and heart.
